Our Story

Handmade from the heart of the Lowcountry

The Junk Bug started with a passion for turning beautiful materials into wearable and decorative art. Inspired by the lush coastal surroundings of the South Carolina Lowcountry, every bag, bracelet, sign, and wreath is a labor of love.


You can find us at local farmers markets across Hilton Head and Bluffton, or place a custom order to get something made just for you. When you buy from The Junk Bug, you're bringing home a little piece of the Lowcountry.
