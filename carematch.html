Who CareMatch Serves

Built for two communities that deserve better

Too many platforms serve neither families nor caregivers well. CareMatch was designed from lived experience to do both — right.

🏠

For Families

Finding a caregiver who truly understands your loved one's disability or chronic illness shouldn't feel like a gamble. CareMatch gives you confidence, not just options.

  • Caregivers vetted for disability competency
  • Filter by condition, care type & location
  • Transparent hourly rates, no surprises
  • Private pay — no insurance hassles
  • Direct messaging with caregivers
  • Dedicated family support team
💙

For Caregivers

Your skill and dedication deserve real respect — fair pay, a supportive community, and clients who genuinely value what you do. CareMatch is built for you too.

  • Free to join, always
  • Fair Pay Standard — minimum rates enforced
  • Connect directly with private-pay families
  • Peer community & continuing education
  • Build your reputation with reviews
  • Flexible — you set your schedule & rates
