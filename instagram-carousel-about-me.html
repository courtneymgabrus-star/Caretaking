My Story

I grew up without representation.

When you live with disability and never see yourself reflected — in media, in medicine, in the systems meant to support you — you learn to fight. Not because you want to. Because you have to.

I didn't see anyone who looked like my life. So I decided to become that person for someone else.

The Work

Years of fighting for the people who make care possible.

Caregivers are the backbone of the disability community — underpaid, undervalued, and invisible to the systems that need them most. I spent years in the trenches advocating to change that.

My Why

Care work changed my life — twice.

🤝

As someone who needed care

Growing up disabled taught me firsthand what it means to rely on others — and how broken the systems around that can be.

✏️

As someone who fought back

I turned that experience into advocacy, community-building, and now — a memoir that puts it all on the page.

📚

As someone who writes it down

Because our stories deserve to be told. Fully, honestly, without apology.
